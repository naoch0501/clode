**Short answer:** You can see the best of South Osaka in one day, almost entirely on foot.
Start at **Tennoji Zoo** in the morning, walk to **Shinsekai** and **Tsutenkaku Tower** for a
kushikatsu lunch, spend the afternoon in the anime and game shops of **Den Den Town / Otaku Road**,
then finish under the neon lights of **Dotonbori**. The whole route is about 4 km from start to finish.

> **Local tip:** [YOUR TIP: このコース全体で一番伝えたいこと。例「動物園は開園直後が一番動物が動いていて空いている」]

The order matters: the zoo opens early, most shops on Otaku Road don't open until late morning,
and Dotonbori is at its best after dark.

## Before you go

- **Getting there:** Take the Midosuji Line or Sakaisuji Line to **Dobutsuen-mae Station**, or JR / Midosuji Line to **Tennoji Station**. [CHECK: 梅田・なんばからの所要時間と運賃]
- **Transport pass:** If you plan to ride the subway several times and visit paid sights, check whether a pass like the [Osaka Amazing Pass](https://www.klook.com/) [AFFILIATE] covers the places you want. [CHECK: 対象施設と料金。通天閣などが含まれるか]
- **Staying connected:** You'll be using maps all day. An [eSIM](https://www.airalo.com/) [AFFILIATE] set up before you land is the easiest option.
- **Comfortable shoes:** You'll walk around 4 km, plus plenty of time on your feet in the zoo and shops.

## 9:30 — Tennoji Zoo

One of Japan's oldest zoos, right next to Tennoji Park. It's compact enough to see in about two
hours, which makes it an easy morning stop.

## 11:30 — Shinsekai and Tsutenkaku Tower

Exit the zoo on the Shinsekai side and you're in one of Osaka's most retro neighbourhoods:
giant fugu lanterns, old-school game arcades and shop signs that haven't changed in decades.
**Tsutenkaku Tower** stands in the middle of it all.

At the observation deck, look for **Billiken**, the "god of things as they ought to be."
Rubbing the soles of his feet is said to bring good luck.

## 12:30 — Kushikatsu lunch in Shinsekai

Shinsekai is the home of **kushikatsu**: deep-fried skewers of meat, seafood and vegetables.
The one rule everyone should know: **no double dipping** in the shared sauce. If you need more
sauce, scoop it on with a piece of cabbage.

[YOUR TIP: 実際に行った店の名前、頼んだもの、値段、英語メニューの有無、並び具合]

- **Budget:** about ¥1,500–2,500 per person [CHECK]

## 14:00 — Den Den Town and Otaku Road

From Shinsekai, walk north for about 15 minutes (or take the Sakaisuji Line one stop to
Ebisucho) and you'll reach **Den Den Town**, Osaka's electronics and otaku district.

The main street, Sakaisuji, has the big electronics and hobby stores. One block west is
**Otaku Road**, a narrower street packed with anime goods, figures, trading cards, retro video
games and maid cafés.

[YOUR TIP: おすすめの店、掘り出し物が見つかる店、秋葉原との違い]

- **Shop hours:** many shops open around 11:00 [CHECK]
- **Tax-free shopping:** Bring your passport. Japan's tax-free rules changed in November 2026 to a refund system, so check how it works at the store before you buy. [CHECK: 免税制度の最新の仕組み]
- **Etiquette:** Some shops don't allow photos inside. Look for signs or ask first.

## 17:30 — Dotonbori at night

From Otaku Road it's about a 10-minute walk to **Dotonbori**. Arrive around sunset, when the
famous Glico Running Man sign and the canal-side neon lights switch on.

- **Street food:** takoyaki, okonomiyaki and kushikatsu stands line the street
- **River cruise:** a short cruise along the Dotonbori canal is a relaxing way to see the lights. [Book a Dotonbori river cruise](https://www.klook.com/) [AFFILIATE] [CHECK: 料金・所要時間]
- **Food tour:** if you'd rather not choose on your own, a [guided street food tour](https://www.getyourguide.com/) [AFFILIATE] takes you to several local spots.

[YOUR TIP: 夜の道頓堀で実際に食べたもの・おすすめ・混雑を避けるコツ]

## Where to stay

Stay near **Namba** and you can walk back from Dotonbori in minutes. Namba also has direct
trains to Kansai Airport.

## FAQ

### Can I do this route in the other direction?

Yes, but you'll arrive at Otaku Road before many shops open and miss Dotonbori's night lights.
The south-to-north order works best.
